# Simple scenes on real songs, 2026-10-31: 0035 measured

Change [0035](../changes/0035-simple-scenes-for-the-story-and-the-feeling.md) against the plans the
2026-10-19 batch made, on four of its songs. The harness and every score are in the private research
repository (study 0016); the pictures are in `~/hone-research/simple-scenes-2026-10/`.

## Question

Does a shot list planned by focus (one face, one figure, a pair, the place, one detail) give pictures
that are cleaner, tell the song's story and carry its feeling better than the shot lists the planner
made before?

## Setup

- **Songs:** *Dancing with a Ghost* (playful, modern), *Zahaak* (myth, dread), *Quiet Morning Pages*
  (calm, instrumental) and *Stay on the Gate* (action, humour), each with its kept bible and approved
  timeline.
- **A:** the song's old shot list (the batch's final, reviewed one). **B:** a 0035 shot list from the
  same bible and timeline (the shot list, the review on Haiku and its repair answered by clean agents).
- **Moments:** fifteen evenly spaced moments per song; at each, A's and B's shot drawn with today's code
  (0034's place-first references), the scene prompts written by clean agents (separate for A and B), the
  subject pictures the batch made reused, one seed each. 120 scenes; none failed.
- **Judging:** Haiku agents, blind, A and B shuffled left and right per moment, given the story, the
  lines sung around the moment and the part's feeling: per picture `clean`, `story`, `feeling`, and which
  of the two better shows that moment of the song with its feeling.

## Conclusions

1. **On real songs the pictures were already clean.** 59 of 60 of the old plans' scenes had no visible
   defect: the hard cases of 0029 and 0034 are rarer in real plans than in the harness. Simpler shots
   cannot gain much there.
2. **The feeling is the same** (62% either way), **the story reads less** (70% against 82%), and the
   judges preferred the old shot a little more often (31 to 27, within noise). 0035 does not meet its own
   bar (at least A on clean and story, better on feeling and in the choice), so it is not made the
   default.
3. **What it lost:** the old plans' two-person shots carried the story (19 of 22 read, 14 of 22 judged
   better); 0035 planned a third as many, and its empty `place` shots read worst (6 of 10 on story, 4 of
   10 on feeling). More faces did not carry more feeling.
4. **Where it won:** the calm instrumental song (10 to 4), where there is no sung action to show and
   simple pictures held the mood.

## Limits

Fifteen moments a song, four songs, one seed a moment, one scorer kind (Haiku), agents as writers;
differences under about five moments of sixty are noise.
